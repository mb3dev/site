Featured Publication

The Esoteric Theology of Philip K. Dick

All books
Edited volume · Bloomsbury Academic · January 2026

This volume commemorates the 50th anniversary of Philip K. Dick's transformative 1974 mystical experiences. Contributing authors examine the enduring significance of PKD through diverse scholarly perspectives engaging with his self-understanding as Christian, gnostic, mystic, and theologian.

Edited with G. Sieg, the collection challenges the misconception of Dick as exclusively a countercultural gnostic — identifying his esoteric Christian gnosis as the basis of an ultimately moral, consistently humanistic theology.
