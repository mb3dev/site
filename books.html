Published Edited volume

The Esoteric Theology of Philip K. Dick

Bloomsbury Academic · with G. Sieg · January 8, 2026

This volume collects chapters written to commemorate the 50th anniversary of Philip K. Dick's transformative 1974 mystical experiences, through which he ultimately contextualized his influential and posthumously much-adapted science fiction. Contributing authors examine the enduring significance of PKD through diverse scholarly perspectives that engage seriously with his self-understanding as Christian, gnostic, mystic, and theologian.

The collection challenges the frequent misconception of Dick as exclusively relevant to countercultural Gnostic mysticism. Instead, his esoteric Christian gnosis is identified and analyzed as the basis of his ultimately moral and consistently humanistic theology.

Under contract Edited volume

The Legend of Zelda & Religion

Bloomsbury Academic · with Talbot Hook · forthcoming 2027

An edited scholarly volume exploring how religion and the sacred emerge from within the structure and narrative of The Legend of Zelda series. Rather than mapping Zelda onto existing traditions, the volume investigates how religious meaning arises internally — through gameplay mechanics, symbolism, spatial design, and narrative. Topics include creation myths, sacred objects, temples and shrines as sacred spaces, time loops as ritual vehicles, sacrifice and vocation, music as sacred technology, and the cultural afterlife of Zelda as mythic text.

Barros, M. C., & Hook, T. (Eds.). (forthcoming 2027). The Legend of Zelda & religion. Bloomsbury Academic.

Under contract Monograph

Philip K. Dick's Omega Point

Anti-Oedipus Press · Exegetics: PKD Studies

This monograph examines Philip K. Dick's engagement with Pierre Teilhard de Chardin's theology — particularly the concept of the Omega Point — drawing primarily from Dick's Exegesis. While Dick references Chardin and the Omega Point several times, the connection remains underexplored in Dickian scholarship. The volume maps the extent of Dick's reading of Chardin and investigates how he makes use of and expands Chardin's eschatological framework.

Barros, M. C. (forthcoming). Philip K. Dick's Omega Point. Anti-Oedipus Press.
