Dissertation: Sensorimotor Content in Dreams and Waking Religious Cognition

Active

National University · Chair: Dr. Patrick McNamara

Examines whether simulation richness in dream content predicts religiosity and paranormal belief. Uses a grounded cognition framework — modeling dreaming as a high-intensity simulation process — and longitudinal dream data to test how variation in simulated experience relates to supernatural agent concepts and belief orientation.

Current: data analysis & chapter drafting

Grounded Cognition Dreams Supernatural Agents Religiosity

The Legend of Zelda & Religion

Active

Bloomsbury Academic · with Talbot Hook · due June 2026

An edited scholarly volume exploring how religion and the sacred emerge from within the structure and narrative of The Legend of Zelda series. Rather than mapping Zelda onto existing traditions, the volume investigates how religious meaning arises internally — through gameplay mechanics, symbolism, spatial design, and narrative. Timed to coincide with the Zelda film debut in March 2027.

Current: second drafts

Zelda Media Studies Myth Sacred Space

Philip K. Dick's Omega Point

Active

Anti-Oedipus Press · Exegetics: PKD Studies

A monograph examining Philip K. Dick's engagement with Pierre Teilhard de Chardin's theology — particularly the concept of the Omega Point — drawing primarily from Dick's Exegesis. The volume maps the extent of Dick's reading of Chardin and investigates how he makes use of and expands Chardin's eschatological framework.

Current: initial research
